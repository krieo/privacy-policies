Privacy Policy

Amplify Audio — Last updated: April 23, 2026

Summary: Audio from your microphone is processed entirely on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects to serve ads, and you control your consent for that.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Amplify Audio ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. Amplify Audio is a real-time audio amplification app developed by Krieo that enhances what you hear through your headphones for situations like lectures, conversations, and everyday listening.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

The App captures audio from your microphone, processes it in real time, and plays the result through your connected headphones. The audio stream is held in memory only long enough to be processed and is then discarded.

  • Audio is not recorded — nothing is saved to disk unless you explicitly use a recording feature
  • Audio is not transmitted — it never leaves your device
  • Audio processing stops immediately when you deactivate amplification or close the App
  • Your in-app preferences (gain level, audio mode, EQ presets, theme, hearing test results) are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore

2.2 Information Collected by Third Parties

Service Data Collected Purpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, device info, approximate location (from IP), ad interaction data Serving ads within the App

If you are in a region covered by GDPR (EEA, UK, Switzerland), CCPA (California), or similar laws, you will see a consent form on first launch where you can choose whether to allow personalised ads. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Options (when available in your region).

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not record, save, or transmit any audio
  • We do not collect your location or GPS data
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs, or SMS
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services
  • Krieo does not operate any servers that receive your data
  • Krieo does not collect device identifiers beyond what Google's AdMob SDK requires

3. How Audio Is Processed

All audio processing — amplification, equalization, noise suppression, echo cancellation, voice isolation, dynamic range compression, and feedback control — runs entirely on your device using the Android audio framework. Audio is held in a small in-memory buffer just long enough to be processed and played back.

We cannot listen to, copy, or otherwise access your microphone audio — not even if you send us a bug report — because nothing is recorded and nothing is transmitted off your device. That is the intended design.

4. Permissions

Amplify Audio asks only for what each feature needs:

Permission Why It's Needed Required?
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Capture the audio stream that is amplified and played back to your headphones Required — this is the core feature
Modify Audio Settings Detect connected headphones and audio output devices to provide safety warnings when no headphones are connected Required
Foreground Service (Microphone) Keep amplification running reliably while the screen is off or the App is in the background Required for background use
Notifications Show the persistent foreground-service notification while amplification is active Required on Android 13+
Bluetooth Detect connected Bluetooth audio devices so we can warn about latency and recommend wired headphones for the best experience Optional
Internet & Network State Loading and serving ads via Google AdMob Required for ads

None of these permissions are used for data collection. You can revoke any optional permission at any time through your device's Settings.

5. Advertising

Amplify Audio shows ads from Google AdMob to keep the app free. Ads appear sparingly — you will not see an ad on every launch. When ads are shown, Google's AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (resettable from your device settings)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • General device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob's policies. Krieo does not receive or store this data.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Ads are never shown while amplification is actively processing audio. They appear only between sessions, when the App returns to the foreground.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • App preferences (gain level, audio mode, EQ settings, theme) are stored using Android's DataStore in the App's private storage
  • No audio is written to disk by the App
  • Krieo does not transmit any data to external servers
  • The App uses Android's standard cloud backup and device-transfer mechanisms for your preferences only — no audio or sensitive data is ever included in a backup

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local preferences: Stored until you clear the App's data or uninstall
  • Audio data: Not retained at all — processed in memory and discarded immediately
  • Uninstalling the App: Removes all local data (preferences, hearing-test results, custom presets) from your device permanently

8. Children's Privacy

Amplify Audio is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the App displays ads via Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers as described above, we do not recommend the App for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, you can reset the device's Advertising ID and uninstall the App to stop any further data collection.

9. Hearing Health Disclaimer

Amplify Audio is a consumer audio tool, not a medical device. It is not a hearing aid and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any hearing condition. The in-app hearing test is for general awareness only and is not a substitute for an audiogram performed by a qualified audiologist. Always use the App at safe listening levels and follow the in-app safety warnings.

10. Third-Party Links

The App may display advertisements that link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change what Amplify Audio collects, this page will be updated and the change will be reflected in the in-app privacy policy in the same release. Material changes will be called out in the app update's release notes.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent for personalised ads (via the in-app consent form or device settings)

Since all in-app data is stored locally on your device, you have full control at all times. No request to us is needed to access or delete your data.
