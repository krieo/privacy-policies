Privacy Policy

Bill Calculator by Krieo

Last updated: 29 April 2026

Krieo (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Bill Calculator mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Information We Do Not Collect

Bill Calculator is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or files.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter in the App is stored locally on your device and never leaves it unless you choose to share it. This includes:

  • Bill calculations (amounts, tip and tax percentages, split details).
  • Person names and item descriptions, if you use the itemized split feature.
  • Optional labels you assign to saved bills.
  • Your theme preference (Light, Dark, Midnight, or System Default).

You can delete individual bills or clear your entire history at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID).
  • IP address.
  • General device information (device model, operating system version).
  • Ad interaction data (impressions and clicks).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can manage your ad preferences through your device’s Google Settings under “Ads”. For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing Your Data

The App includes a share feature that lets you send a text summary of a bill calculation via your preferred app (such as messaging, email, or social media). This action is entirely user-initiated and we have no access to the data you share.

If you contact us via the “Report a Bug” or “Request a Feature” options in Settings, your email client will open with a pre-filled draft. No data is sent until you choose to send the email yourself.

Device Permissions

The App requires only the following permissions:

  • Internet access – Required to display advertisements.
  • Network state – Used to check connectivity for loading ads.

The App does not request access to your camera, location, contacts, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive permissions.

Device Backup

If you have device backup enabled (e.g. Google One or your device manufacturer’s backup service), your system may include the App’s locally stored data as part of the backup. This is controlled by your device’s backup settings, not by the App.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect any personal data, no special provisions are required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the App and the updated date will be noted at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
